Homeserver Backup

Homeserver Backup is a private, self-hosted backup integration used by its operator to store encrypted backups in the operator's own Google Drive account.

The integration is for personal use only and is not offered as a public service. It uses Google OAuth to authorize access to Google Drive for uploading, verifying, listing and managing encrypted backup files.

Backup files are encrypted locally before being uploaded. The application does not provide third-party accounts or accept public uploads.

For details on data handling, read the Privacy Policy.

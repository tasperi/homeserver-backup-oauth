Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Purpose and operator

Homeserver Backup is a private, self-hosted integration operated for the operator's own backups. It is not a public service and does not accept accounts from external users.

Google user data accessed

With the operator's authorization, the integration accesses Google Drive to create, upload, list, read, verify, and delete backup files and folders. It does not access Gmail, contacts, or other Google services.

Use and storage of data

Backup files are encrypted locally before transfer to Google Drive. The encrypted files are stored in the operator's Google Drive account. OAuth tokens and encryption credentials are held on the operator's private systems, not on this website. Google processes files stored in Google Drive under Google's applicable terms and privacy policies.

Sharing and disclosure

The operator does not sell Google user data or share it with advertisers. Data is transferred to Google Drive solely to provide the operator's backup and recovery functionality.

Retention and deletion

The operator manages backup retention and can delete backup files from Google Drive. Revoking the application's Google access stops future access but does not automatically delete existing backups; these can be removed separately by the operator.

Security

Backup content is encrypted before upload. This informational website does not collect backup files, OAuth credentials, or user-submitted personal information.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, contact the operator through the repository issue tracker. Do not post passwords, access tokens, or private backup information in public issues.
